About the Image to JPG Converter

This tool converts images such as PNG and WebP into JPG. JPG is widely supported and usually gives small files, which makes it a good choice for photos.

Tips for best results

  • JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas cannot be kept.
  • Photos suit JPG well; logos and screenshots with sharp text may look better as PNG.

Common questions about this tool

Why convert an image to JPG?

Many websites, forms and apps accept JPG, and JPG files are usually smaller than PNG for photos.

Will I lose quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so a small quality loss is possible.
