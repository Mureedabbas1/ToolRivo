QR Code Scanner

Upload an image containing a QR code to read the link or text. Image decoding happens in your browser; your selected image is not uploaded to ToolRivo.

Scan a QR code from an image

Choose an image to begin.

How to use the QR scanner

  1. Choose an image that contains a visible QR code.
  2. Click Scan QR Code and wait for the result.
  3. Copy the decoded text or link. Check links before opening them.

Privacy and safety

The selected image is processed locally in your browser. QR codes can contain unsafe links; review the decoded address before visiting it. The scanner reads QR codes from images and does not use your camera.
