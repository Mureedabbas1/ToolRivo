How to use Word Counter

A word counter helps check essay length, article size, captions and other writing before you publish or submit it. Counts update as you type, so you can compare word and character limits without uploading your text.

  1. Paste or type text into the text box.
  2. The tool counts words, total characters, characters excluding whitespace and sentence endings.
  3. Review the live counts as you edit.
  4. Copy your text when finished; the text stays in your browser.

About the Word Counter

The Word Counter counts words, characters and sentences in your text as you type. It helps with essays, social posts, product descriptions and any text with a length limit.

Tips for best results

  • Check whether your limit counts characters with or without spaces.
  • Different tools can count some words slightly differently, such as hyphenated words.

Common questions about this tool

How are words counted?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.

Is there a text length limit?

Very long text may be slower on older devices, but there is no fixed limit set by the tool.
