About the BMI Calculator

BMI (Body Mass Index) compares your weight with your height. It is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared, and gives a quick screening number.

Tips for best results

  • Use your current weight and your height in the units the tool asks for.
  • BMI does not measure body fat or muscle, so treat it as a rough guide only.

Common questions about this tool

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. It is a general screening number. Talk to a doctor for health advice.

Why can BMI be misleading?

It ignores muscle mass, age and body shape, so athletic people can show a high BMI without excess body fat.
