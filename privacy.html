Privacy Policy

How ToolRivo handles information when you use our online tools.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

ToolRivo provides browser-based utilities for documents, images, text, QR codes, calculations and other everyday tasks. This policy explains the main ways information may be handled when you visit the website or use a tool.

1. Information you provide to a tool

The information you enter depends on the tool you choose. Some tools process input in your browser. Other features, such as network or website checks, may send the relevant query to the service being checked or to a third-party provider. Processing can differ by tool, so read the instructions shown on the individual tool before submitting information. Do not enter confidential or sensitive material unless you have verified that the tool is suitable for it.

2. Files and browser processing

Where a tool works locally, selected files are processed by code running in your browser. Some tools may load libraries or services from third parties, and not every tool necessarily follows the same processing path. ToolRivo does not promise that every operation is entirely local or that every third-party library has the same privacy practices. Review the specific tool and any third-party service notices before use.

3. Technical information and analytics

Like most websites, the hosting platform and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver, secure and maintain the site, such as browser, device, request and error information. ToolRivo uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and improve the service. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information.

4. Advertising

ToolRivo may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise ads, subject to your settings and applicable policies. Learn more in Google's advertising policy and manage available ad-personalisation choices through My Ad Center.

5. Third-party services and links

Some tools rely on external libraries, APIs, or linked websites. When you use those features, the relevant third party may receive information required to provide the requested function and may handle it under its own privacy policy. ToolRivo does not control third-party privacy practices.

6. Retention and security

Information handled by a browser-based tool may remain in the browser session or device until you close the page, clear browser data, or remove downloaded files. Information sent to an external service is subject to that service's handling and retention rules. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children

ToolRivo is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

You can stop using a tool at any time, avoid submitting sensitive information, manage browser cookies, and review Google's advertising and privacy settings. Your available rights may depend on your location and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, tools or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the ToolRivo contact page.
