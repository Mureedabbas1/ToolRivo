Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to ToolRivo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that may be collected when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

ToolRivo provides online tools that are designed to work directly in your web browser. Many tools process files or text locally in your browser and do not require you to create an account.

ToolRivo does not currently use a dedicated Google Analytics property. Hosting providers may process standard technical request and security logs needed to deliver and protect the website.

2. Files and Text

When you use tools such as image compressors, image converters, calculators or text tools, files and text may be processed directly in your browser where technically possible.

You should avoid entering passwords, confidential documents, financial information or other sensitive information into any public online tool.

3. Cookies

ToolRivo integrates Google AdSense. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising measurement, security and website functionality, subject to available settings and applicable law.

Google AdSense is integrated into the website, although whether ads are served depends on Google's review, account settings and ad availability. When ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize where permitted, and measure ads. Where required, advertising consent must be collected using a Google-certified consent management platform configured in AdSense. Check the actual consent configuration in the AdSense account; this policy text alone does not implement a consent banner.

4. Third-Party Services

Some ToolRivo features load third-party libraries from content delivery networks (CDNs). For example, the QR Code Generator loads a JavaScript library to generate QR codes in your browser. YouTube thumbnail images may also be loaded from YouTube's image servers.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing their policies when using those services.

5. Analytics

ToolRivo does not currently use a dedicated Google Analytics property. The hosting provider may process standard technical logs for website delivery, security and reliability. This policy will be updated if analytics services are enabled in the future.

6. Advertising

ToolRivo includes Google AdSense integration, but advertisements may only appear when Google serves them and availability may depend on Google's review and settings. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize where permitted, limit, and measure advertisements. Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy information and manage available ad-personalization settings through Google.

7. External Links

ToolRivo may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolRivo is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the website.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact page of ToolRivo.
