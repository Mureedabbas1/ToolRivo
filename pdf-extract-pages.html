ToolRivo Learning Guide

How to Extract Specific Pages from a PDF

You do not need to send an entire report when someone only needs a few pages. Extracting pages creates a new PDF containing just the pages you select, which is useful for forms, chapters, receipts, and supporting documents.

How to use this tool

  1. Select your PDF file.
  2. Enter the page numbers to keep, separated by commas, such as 1,3,5.
  3. Choose Extract Pages and download the smaller PDF. Check the page order before sharing it.

Helpful tips

  • Use page numbers as they appear in the PDF viewer, starting from page 1.
  • Keep the numbers within the document's page count.
  • If you need to remove pages rather than keep selected pages, use the Remove PDF Pages tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Enter a comma-separated list such as 1,3,5 to keep separate pages in one new PDF.

Will the original PDF change?

No. The tool creates a separate output file; keep your original as a backup.
