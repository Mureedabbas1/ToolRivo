ToolRivo Learning Guide

How to Convert JPG Images into a PDF

Converting photos to PDF is useful for submitting scanned paperwork, combining receipts, creating a photo handout, or sharing multiple images as one document. A single PDF can be easier to email and organize than many image attachments.

How to use this tool

  1. Select one or more JPG or PNG images.
  2. Check the selected images and their intended order before converting.
  3. Click Convert to PDF, then download and review the new document.

Helpful tips

  • Use clear, well-lit scans so text remains readable in the final PDF.
  • Large photos can create a large PDF file; compress the finished PDF if you need to meet an upload limit.
  • Keep original images if you may need to crop or edit them later.
