How to use Unix Timestamp Converter

Unix timestamps represent time as seconds or milliseconds from 1 January 1970 UTC. Converting them can help when checking logs, APIs, databases and application events.

  1. Choose Show current timestamp to see the current seconds and milliseconds values, or enter a timestamp and select Convert timestamp.
  2. The tool detects seconds versus milliseconds and displays a UTC date.

About the Unix Timestamp Converter

The Unix Timestamp Converter shows the current Unix time and converts timestamps to dates. A Unix timestamp counts the seconds since 1 January 1970 (UTC) and is used in databases, logs and APIs.

Tips for best results

  • 10-digit timestamps are usually seconds and 13-digit ones are usually milliseconds.
  • Check the time zone when comparing a timestamp with a local date.

Common questions about this tool

What is a Unix timestamp?

It is the number of seconds that have passed since 1 January 1970 at 00:00 UTC.

Why is my converted date wrong?

The value may be in milliseconds instead of seconds, or shown in a different time zone.
