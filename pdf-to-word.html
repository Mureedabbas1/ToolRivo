How to Convert PDF to Word?

ToolRivo's PDF to Word converter lets you extract text from a PDF and save it as an editable Word document.

  • Click "Choose PDF File".
  • Select your PDF document.
  • Click "Convert to Word".
  • Wait for the conversion to finish.
  • Download your Word document.

Important Note

This converter extracts selectable text from PDF files. Scanned or image-based PDFs may not convert correctly because they require OCR technology.
