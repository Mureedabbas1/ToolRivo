How to use JPG to PNG Converter

Convert a supported image file to PNG in your browser. PNG is useful when you need lossless image output or transparency support; a JPG source itself does not contain transparency.

  2. Wait for the conversion and choose Download PNG.
  3. Very large images may take longer and use more device memory.

About the JPG to PNG Converter

This tool converts JPG images to PNG. PNG is a lossless format that keeps sharp edges and supports transparency in new images.

Tips for best results

  • Converting JPG to PNG does not restore detail already lost in the JPG.
  • PNG files are usually larger than JPG files for photos.

Common questions about this tool

Does JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. It only changes the format. Quality lost earlier cannot be recovered.

When is PNG better than JPG?

For graphics, logos, screenshots and images that need sharp edges or transparency.
