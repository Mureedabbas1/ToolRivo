Text to PDF Converter

Type or paste your text, create a PDF, and download it. The converter runs in your browser.

Plain text only. For complex layouts, images, or non-Latin scripts, review the downloaded PDF before using it.

How to convert text to PDF

  1. Paste or type your text in the box above.
  2. Select Create and Download PDF.
  3. Open the downloaded file and check its contents.

Frequently asked questions

Is this text-to-PDF converter free?

Yes. It is free to use in a modern browser.

Are my words uploaded?

The text is processed in your browser by the PDF library; this page does not send the text to a server.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes, on modern mobile browsers. Longer documents may be easier to edit on a larger screen.
