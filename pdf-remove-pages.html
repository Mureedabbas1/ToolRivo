ToolRivo Learning Guide

How to Delete Unwanted Pages from a PDF

Blank scans, duplicate pages, and irrelevant sections can make a PDF harder to use. Removing selected pages helps you create a concise document before sending it, uploading it, or printing it.

How to use this tool

  1. Select the PDF file.
  2. Enter the page numbers to delete, separated by commas, for example 2,5,7.
  3. Click Remove Pages, download the result, and verify that the intended pages were removed.

Helpful tips

  • Double-check the page numbers before running the tool; page 1 means the first page in the PDF.
  • Keep a copy of the original in case you remove a page by mistake.
  • To save only a few selected pages instead, use Extract PDF Pages.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove several pages at once?

Yes. Enter multiple page numbers separated by commas.

Will deleted pages be recoverable from the new file?

They are omitted from the output file. Keep the original PDF if you might need those pages later.
