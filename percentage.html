About the Percentage Calculator

The Percentage Calculator answers common questions such as what is X% of Y, what percent one number is of another, and how much a value changed in percent. It is useful for discounts, marks, tax and price changes.

Tips for best results

  • Percentage change is (new value - old value) divided by the old value, times 100.
  • A discount of 20% on 50 means 20% of 50 is 10, so the final price is 40.

Common questions about this tool

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example 15% of 200 is 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.
